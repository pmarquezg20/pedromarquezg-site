## What 학 (學) means

학 (學, *hak*) is the root of "study" and "learning". It is one of the first Sino-Korean roots you meet, because it sits inside the word for school (학교) and the word for student (학생). Once you see it, a whole family of school vocabulary stops looking like separate words to memorize.

The hanja 學 is a hook only. You don't have to write it. Just notice that every word below has 학 in it and that every one of them is about studying, schools or students.

**Root:** 학 (學), pronounced *hak*. **Core meaning:** study, learning.

## 10 Korean words with 학

| Word | Romanization | Meaning | Hanja |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 학교 | hakgyo | school | 學校 |
| 학생 | haksaeng | student | 學生 |
| 입학 | iphak | admission to a school, entering a school | 入學 |
| 방학 | banghak | school break, vacation from school | 放學 |
| 유학 | yuhak | studying abroad | 留學 |
| 대학 | daehak | university, college | 大學 |
| 학년 | hangnyeon | school year, grade (as in 1st year) | 學年 |
| 학원 | hagwon | private academy, cram school | 學院 |
| 학비 | hakbi | tuition and school expenses | 學費 |
| 학습 | hakseup | learning, study (formal) | 學習 |

I did not add TOPIK levels to this table. Many of these words show up in TOPIK I and II materials, but I have not checked each one against the official lists, so I'd rather leave it out than guess.

## How the root works

Notice the pattern: 학 can come first (학교, 학생, 학원) or last (입학, 방학, 유학). In both positions it keeps the same idea of studying. Also notice that 대학 is itself 大 (big) + 學 (study): the "big school" is the university. See the [대 page](/korean/roots/dae) for that root.

## 3 example sentences

1. **저는 서울에서 한국어 학교에 다녀요.**  
   I go to a Korean language school in Seoul.
2. **방학에 친구가 한국에 놀러 와요.**  
   My friend is coming to visit Korea during the break.
3. **이 학원은 저녁에만 수업이 있어요.**  
   This academy only has classes in the evening.

## Memory tip

Link 학 to the sound of "hack" as in "I'm going to hack this subject". It's silly, but 학 is a closed syllable that ends in a hard k, so "hak" really does sound like that. Then attach it to one anchor word, 학교, and every other word becomes "a thing connected to school".

## How to study these words

Don't memorize the ten words as one block. Take two words today, say each one out loud, and write your own short sentence with it. Tomorrow, cover the English column and try to recall the meaning from the Korean alone. When you meet a new word with 학, guess its meaning from the root first, then check a dictionary. A wrong guess teaches more than a lookup, and after a week the root starts to feel familiar instead of foreign.
